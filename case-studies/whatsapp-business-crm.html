Project Overview

A major retail brand with over 200 outlets across India struggled to maintain personal connections with their customers. Their SMS campaigns had low open rates (less than 10%), and customer support was overwhelmed with repetitive queries.

GryphalCode implemented a unified WhatsApp Business CRM integrated with their existing ERP. We developed automated chatbots for order tracking, FAQs, and a specialized dashboard for human agents to handle complex queries.

The Challenge

  • Declining customer re-engagement via traditional channels.
  • No centralized view of customer conversations across outlets.
  • High latency in responding to customer queries.

The Solution

Leveraging the WhatsApp Business API, we created a highly interactive CRM experience. Post-purchase satisfaction surveys and personalized promotion blasts were automated, while the agent dashboard used AI to suggest best responses.

Key Results

The results were transformative: 85% open rates on messages and a 4.2x increase in customer re-engagement . Over 90% of basic queries are now resolved by the chatbot without human intervention.
